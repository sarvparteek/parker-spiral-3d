Things to try

  1. Set λ to 0 and click Side (x-z). Why does the spiral turn into a straight line? Then click Top (x-y).
  2. Keep the cone surface on and sweep λ from 0 to 90. What does the cone turn into at each end? What do those two shapes look like from the side?
  3. Set λ to 80 and r to 1. Read the distance to the axis and the height. Which trig function of λ gives each one? Which wall shows which?
  4. Fix V and note ψ at λ = 0, 45 and 80. Divide each tan ψ by the value at λ = 0. What do you get?
  5. Press Play rotation. Each parcel moves straight outward and never changes longitude. Why do the parcels still end up lying along a spiral? Why is that spiral looser at high latitude? (Think about how fast the footpoint moves.)

Terms

  1. Latitude λ: angle measured up from the Sun's equator. 0° is the equator, 90° is the pole.
  2. Rotation axis: the z-axis here. The Sun spins about it once every 25.4 days (sidereal).
  3. Footpoint: where a field line is anchored on the Sun. It turns with the Sun.
  4. Garden-hose angle ψ: the angle between the field line and the straight-out (radial) direction at a given point.
